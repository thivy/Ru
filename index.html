3D Rubik's Cube Solver

Upload 6 photos → check colors → follow the steps on the 3D cube

1 Upload all 6 sides

Select all six photos at once, in any order. Each photo should show one face filling most of the picture (you can drag the grid on a photo and resize it).

Best way to take the photos

Hold the cube with the white center on top and the green center facing you. Photograph: top, front, right, back, left and bottom. For the four side faces keep the white edge at the top of the photo; for the top face keep green toward the bottom of the photo; for the bottom face keep green toward the top. Faces photographed in a different rotation are corrected automatically.

2 Check the detected colors

Compare with your real cube. Click any sticker to change its color (centers are fixed).

3 Follow the steps

Hold your cube with the white center on top and the green center facing you, exactly like the 3D cube. Press Next for each move.

Drag to rotate the cube
